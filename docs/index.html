How it works

  1. Paste the shoot folder link

    Copy the link to the Google Drive folder you shared with your client. PhotoPicker searches subfolders too.

  2. Paste the names your client picked

    Commas, spaces or new lines all work. A name without an extension, like IMG_0452, brings the JPEG and the RAW file. Anything it can't find is listed so you can ask your client.

  3. Download everything at once

    Choose a folder on your computer. Files download several at a time. If your connection drops, run it again and finished files are skipped.

Installing and signing in

Windows

Run PhotoPicker-Setup.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", select More info, then Run anyway. This appears for apps from small publishers that have not yet bought a signing certificate.

Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag PhotoPicker into Applications.
  2. Open PhotoPicker. macOS will block it the first time.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and select Open Anyway next to PhotoPicker.

Not sure which Mac you have? Choose the Apple menu, then About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M1" (or M2, M3 and so on) means Apple silicon. "Processor: Intel" means Intel.

First sign-in

Select Sign in with Google. Your browser opens, you choose your account and allow read-only access to Google Drive. PhotoPicker can't change or delete your files.

If Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app", select Advanced, then Go to PhotoPicker.

Your photos stay between Google and you

PhotoPicker runs on your computer. Files go straight from Google Drive to the folder you choose. The developer never receives your photos, file names or Google data. Read the full privacy policy.
