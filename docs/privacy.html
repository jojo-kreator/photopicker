Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

PhotoPicker is a desktop app that helps photographers find and download the photos their clients selected from a Google Drive folder. This page explains what the app does with your Google account and your files. The app is published by the developer.

What PhotoPicker can access

When you sign in with Google, you allow PhotoPicker to view the files in your Google Drive (the read-only Drive permission). PhotoPicker uses this access for two things only:

  • to list the files inside the folder whose link you paste, so it can match them against the file names you paste, and
  • to download the files that match, to the folder on your computer that you choose.

PhotoPicker cannot change, move, share or delete anything in your Drive.

Where your data goes

  • Your photos, file names and folder contents go directly between Google and your computer. They do not pass through any server run by the developer.
  • The developer does not receive, store, sell or share your Google data, your files or your file names.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • The sign-in token that keeps you signed in is stored on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage where available.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PhotoPicker's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Select Sign out in the app to delete the stored sign-in from your computer.
  • You can withdraw PhotoPicker's access at any time in your Google Account, under Security, then Third-party apps with account access.
  • On Windows, uninstalling the app also removes its stored sign-in. On a Mac, select Sign out before you delete the app.
  • Files you downloaded stay in the folder you chose until you delete them.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. GitHub and Google may record standard visit information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. The site itself uses no cookies and no analytics.
